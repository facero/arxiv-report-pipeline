Research interest in High Energy Astrophysics focusing on supernova remnants (both young and evolved) and their non-thermal emission mechanisms, pulsar wind nebulae with emission from radio to TeV energies, and galactic PeVatrons. Active with gamma-ray observatories (Fermi-LAT, HESS, CTA/LST-1) for spectral and morphological studies of SNRs and PWNe. Also engaged in X-ray analysis techniques (hyperspectral imaging, source separation with XMM-Newton, NuSTAR, Chandra) and multi-wavelength studies of supernova remnants, pulsars, and galaxy clusters.
